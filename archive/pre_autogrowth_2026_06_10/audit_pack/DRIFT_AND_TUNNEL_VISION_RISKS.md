## Highest-Risk Pattern

The central risk is not that the architecture is fake. The central risk is that the architecture is real, elaborate, and safety-conscious, but it keeps moving sideways into more instrumentation instead of upward into measurable KRK competence.

The most important falsification is simple:

1. Pick a withheld KRK competence slice before changing code.
2. Freeze all scaffolding not needed for the test.
3. Add or activate one learned structural mechanism.
4. Compare against the protected baseline.
5. Require a measurable conversion improvement with zero protected regressions.

If that cannot be done, the branch is probably bloating around the problem rather than converging on it.
