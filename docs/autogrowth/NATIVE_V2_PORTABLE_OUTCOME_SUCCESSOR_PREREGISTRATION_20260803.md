  serialization, or launch-schema defect may be preserved and minimally
  repaired inside this same package.
- Any semantic organism, candidate, topology, row, package, or result-association
  difference stops before science and is not repairable here.
- Once science starts, a caught program failure is terminal. An external
  interruption may resume only through the same frozen command and journal,
  executing the next unfinished seed only.
- No replacement seed, rediscovery, exposure replay, threshold change, or
  post-result tuning is permitted.
