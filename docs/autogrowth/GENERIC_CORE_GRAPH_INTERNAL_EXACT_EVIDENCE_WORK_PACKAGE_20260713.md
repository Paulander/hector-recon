# Generic-Core Graph-Internal Exact Evidence Work Package

Date: 2026-07-13. Track: generic-core science. Status: frozen before
implementation or fresh execution.

## Prior result, hypothesis, and strongest null

The admitted activation-deficit package is an immutable valid negative. All 80
target structures existed in each request arm, but only 1/20 directed tasks
adequately supported and matured all four targets; 19/80 targets died
unsupported. The request proxy counted trial activations while adjudication
counted matching records retained in a bounded Algorithm-R reservoir. Priority
was mostly unidentified because 86.4% of request opportunities had one
requester. Five fixed-reference policies also drifted; retention remains a
separate factor.

Hypothesis: when a trial candidate exposes its exact retained anonymous rent
support through a real internal `EVIDENCE_DEFICIT` terminal and a graph-local
request SCRIPT, it acquires enough ordinary evidence to survive and mature more
reliably than the identical topology driven by activation-count support, without
creating reward, changing exploration volume, or leaking target semantics.

Strongest null: exact currency does not improve paired minimum retained support,
unsupported deaths, or maturation at the frozen dose; any apparent improvement
depends on graph/RNG/identity mismatch, host-side candidate inspection, changed
lifecycle grace, or insufficiently identified priority.

## Learner/laboratory boundary and changed factor

The learner may receive anonymous active atoms, legal actions, ordinary outcome,
graph state, and one candidate-local internal deficit measurement. It may not
receive target identity, cue meaning, action role, changed mapping, correctness,
evaluation results, stage labels, or post-hoc target reconstruction.

The primary factor is the measurement source behind identical graph request
topology: `activation_count` proxy versus exact current Algorithm-R support.
Maximum-deficit versus shuffled responsibility is a separate, conditional
secondary factor. Fixed reference and ordinary rent are controls. Exploration
schedule/rate, support threshold 32, two-review grace, proposal cadence,
capacity, topology and learning budgets, main/support RNG streams, outcome
learning, and phase-0 admission are frozen to the admitted predecessor.

The graph terminal and candidate request SCRIPT emit request strength. A generic
host actuator bus may collect those emissions and execute the corresponding
already-scheduled exploration action. It may not reread activation or support
fields, infer targets, or connect request nodes to exploitative action-score
roots. Only trials may emit requests. Mature candidates retain counters for
audit but cannot request; pruned candidates are disconnected or disabled and
cannot affect selection.

## Exact measurement and mutation contract

One content-blind helper,
`record_supports_candidate(record, action_id, immutable_members)`, defines both
full `candidate_rent_stats` support and incremental accounting. A reservoir
mutation contains `attempted_record`, `retained`, `inserted_record`,
`evicted_record`, and `retained_index`. Append and replacement retain an inserted
record; Algorithm-R rejection sets retained false and every insertion/eviction/
index field to `None`. The return must preserve prior reservoir contents,
replacement counts, hashes, and RNG-call counts for fixed seeds.

Each candidate initializes `rent_evidence_support` by scanning all pre-birth
reservoir records. Every retained insertion and eviction updates it. Trials and
mature candidates remain audited; support loss through eviction reactivates a
trial deficit. Proposal, review, clone/checkpoint, restore, and finalization
compare incremental support with a fresh scan and hard-fail on mismatch.

## Independently adjudicated gates

1. **Measurement integrity:** zero counter/full-scan mismatches, graph identity
   mismatches, RNG/event-budget mismatches, request firewall failures, stale
   references, root connections, clone/restore mismatches, or omitted final
   terminal-return summaries in every arm.
2. **Evidence acquisition:** all four targets reach exact support 32 in at least
   16/20 exact-directed tasks; exact-directed has at most 4/80 target unsupported
   deaths; and exact-directed exceeds activation-directed paired per-task minimum
   retained support in at least 14/20 with median advantage at least 12.
3. **Maturation:** all four post-hoc target candidates both obtain positive rent
   and mature in at least 16/20 exact-directed tasks.
4. **Priority exposure:** identified only if canonical exact arms have at least
   20% multi-request opportunities, at least 10% unequal-strength opportunities,
   and at least 100 opportunities where the allocators could differ. Otherwise
   report `not_identified`, not equivalence.
5. **Priority effect (conditional):** when exposure is identified,
   exact-directed exceeds exact-shuffled paired minimum retained support in at
   least 14/20 with median advantage at least 12 and paired minimum old/new
   behavior in at least 14/20 with median advantage at least 0.10.
6. **Behavior:** exact-directed median old and new joint success are each at
   least 0.85, at least 16/20 tasks master both, and its paired minimum old/new
   difference versus fixed reference has median at least -0.05.
7. **Stability:** fixed reference median old and new joint success are each at
   least 0.85 and at least 16/20 tasks master both. Exact-directed must also meet
   the behavior retention requirement; no consolidation repair is allowed here.

Evidence-mechanism support requires measurement integrity, evidence acquisition,
and maturation. Full transfer authorization additionally requires behavior,
stability, and an identified passing priority effect. Passing some gates cannot
be reported as passing another. The ordinary arm is descriptive negative-control
replication, not a binding prerequisite for exact-currency support.

## Kill criteria, compute budget, and interpretations

Budget: one implementation and runner; retired/truncated smoke only; planted
diagnostic; full suite; one capped 40-candidate admission with manifest pause;
then at most twenty admitted checkpoints by five phase-1 arms. Persist after
every candidate, seed, and arm. Resume only from parity-proven checkpoints.

Any integrity failure stops execution. After fresh phase 1 begins, thresholds,
seeds, lifecycle, mechanisms, and row pools are immutable. If exact support stays
below 32, close exact-currency requests at this dose and isolate
support-conditioned lifecycle grace next. If support reaches 32 without rent,
isolate individual versus cooperative contribution. If candidates mature
without behavior, investigate action competition/plasticity. If behavior passes
but old skills drift, isolate consolidation/stability. Only if terminal,
maturation, behavior, stability, and conditional priority gates all pass may the
project prepare frame-aware handover and return to native R1. R2 remains closed.

Virtual frames are explicitly excluded from this experiment. After closure, a
design note and deterministic canary may test frame-local `CHILD_RESPONSE` and
`PREDICTION_SURPRISE` with side-effect and self-credit firewalls, but no fresh KRK
claim may be made from that canary.
