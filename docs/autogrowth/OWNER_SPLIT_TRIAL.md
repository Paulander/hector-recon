## Why this checkpoint matters

Weight learning already coexists with owner splitting and local condition birth.
This increment adds provisional acceptance to splitting, reusing TRIAL/MATURE,
local candidate statistics, the internal permission terminal and actual assigned
outcome records. It does not turn on the old permanent pattern-sensor subsystem.
The hope is less premature fragmentation and better training/retention. The risk
is interaction between learning, exploration, birth timing and finite trial
evidence. This tests the combined rule, not an isolated causal effect of maturity.

Already established: whole-owner splits preserve inherited scores and isolate
parameters between owners; impossible new births can be rejected without changing
the observed learning trajectory, saving physical nodes. Some fresh runs acquire
and hold 16/16, but within-owner interference remains. Eight owners did not reliably
help and delayed useful scoring growth. Recombination/merging and shared-trunk
factoring are not implemented. Generic nested composition discovery is separate.

## Declared law, before performance play

At the unchanged 64 actual visits to an active scorer, run ordinary retirement
and one ordinary scoring-birth opportunity first. A supported residual split may
then be nominated. The new condition, if any, starts at zero. Both split children
inherit independent copies of the continuing parent's entire current scorer.
Inherited condition histories remain recorded; new child-local evidence starts
empty. A useful parent is not itself evidence that separation helps.

A local internal RNG assigns unsplit versus split with probability 1/2 before
each action. Internal permission leaves gate the two alternatives; formal graph
confirmations identify the active owner and the graph chooses the real action.
Only that owner receives its actual scalar feedback. No unexecuted action gets
credit. The continuing parent and children keep learning and growing on their own
actual visits. No pending trial blocks ordinary condition growth.

Each trial ends after **256 actual requests in its region**, with no early stop
or score-based extension. Accept iff all of these fixed empirical criteria hold:

- At least 48 actual outcomes from each alternative and 16 from each child.
- Split minus unsplit mean scalar reward is at least 0.10 over the full window.
- The mean reward difference is positive in each fixed 128-request half.

These are exploratory acceptance settings, not a calibrated significance test,
an iid confidence claim, or proof of permanent usefulness. With +1/-1 rewards,
0.10 mean gain corresponds to five percentage points of actual success. Adaptive
policies share a changing schedule; randomization measures access value along
that history, not the counterfactual result of full-time training each topology.
False acceptance/rejection remains possible. Prediction residuals only nominate;
they cannot by themselves earn acceptance. No validation/evaluation score enters.

An accepted split records MATURE on the split hypothesis and retires the continuing
parent into ancestry. Condition states do not automatically mature. Weights remain
plastic and later local splitting remains possible. An unaccepted trial records
PRUNED with reason **unproven at window end**, preserves all outcome/condition
history, and retains the parent's continuing learned scorer. PRUNED is not proof
the distinction is useless. A tested identical partition is not reintroduced
with empty history in that same owner; Boolean complementary tests share identity.
Pending trials at the experiment endpoint remain pending, without extrapolation.

At most one trial involves a given owner, with no nested trial while either
alternative is pending. Disjoint owners can have independent trials. The four
owner ceiling counts the partition if all pending trials were accepted. Each
pending trial's retained parent adds real storage, included in the unchanged
64-parameter, 256-definition, 2048-node, depth-four budgets. Report committed,
reserved and stored scorers separately. No free duplicate parameters or rewards.
