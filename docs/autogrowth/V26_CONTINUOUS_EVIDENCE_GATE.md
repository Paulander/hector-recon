## Frozen question

Hypothesis: resetting an unchanged hypothesis at graph allocation needlessly
delays useful outward competence; preserving its genuinely prospective evidence
will remove that delay without forgetting applicable counterexamples.
Strong null: this bookkeeping delay is not the functional bottleneck; reply
coverage, feature specificity or downstream action/value transfer still prevent
mate-in-2 performance after the ledger is repaired.

The exact canary predicts certification after eight observations in baseline
versus five in repair. Natural training need not produce more certificates:
retaining known negatives may correctly prevent old unsafe ones. The frozen
transfer test is exhaustive evaluation on the runner's seed-defined disjoint
development validation/regression pools, unchanged between arms. No evaluation
answer enters budding, certification, action selection or training rewards.

## Intervention

An unchanged cheap candidate and its later graph cell represent **one
hypothesis**, not two successive discoveries. Record its predicate, typed
roles, immutable source policy, matching semantics and actual construction
frontier at birth. Exclude that event and all earlier events from positive
certification. Four distinct, matching, strictly later positive REAL receipts
may qualify it. Physical graph materialization preserves this ledger instead
of resetting it. It does not change the predicate after seeing those outcomes.

A known applicable failure remains a constraint even when it precedes a new
incarnation. This is local to the same source and predicate, not a global
negative veto. The ecology additionally remembers base-discovery failures;
base-discovery positives are not imported as new training or certification.
Refinement is a new hypothesis with a fresh prospective cut.
Birth records confer no availability. TD still completes before structural
settlement, so the event that completes certification cannot bootstrap itself.

The protocol is explicit and opt-in:

- CLI: `--continuous-hypothesis-evidence` (baseline: the `--no-…` form).
- Curriculum: `r0_boundary_continuous_evidence`.
- Ecology: `continuous_evidence`.

Legacy is the default. No saved V25 certificate is retroactively installed or
rewritten. The snapshot fingerprint distinguishes protocols. Candidate birth
commitments, imported evidence, chronological replay and local source matching
are checked by the authority, not merely trusted from the ecology report.
After the first continuous birth activates the protocol, newly allocated
deferred children also bind exact source/roles and must avoid all already-known
applicable failures. Cells predating that activation retain their legacy
contract; distinguish their providers from continuous-bound providers.

No change to action selection, Q/M3/fast-credit learning rates, curriculum
features, rewards, protected R0 policy, all-reply evaluation, or reply policy is
part of this intervention. This tests the combined evidence-continuity and
retained-counterexample repair; it does not isolate their individual effects.

## Gates

1. Exact synthetic tests: discovery exclusion, transfer of four future
   witnesses, historical contradiction, source/role mismatch, forged birth,
   snapshot/replay and failed-transaction rollback.
2. Code-defined tiny chess fixture: compare the old eight-observation window
   with one birth plus four future observations; no same-event handoff, then
   a real handoff and positive successor credit. Repeated geometry in this
   test is not held-out generalization evidence.
3. Same fresh DEVELOPMENT seed `2026090110`, baseline and repair in separate
   directories; same frozen source, pools and R0 policy. `follow-through`
   profile, at most 32 R1 epochs, wall budget 7,200 seconds per process,
   4,096-MiB peak-RSS ceiling per process, one numerical thread per process.
   Wall/RSS ceilings are checked at complete epochs, not hard deadlines; final
   reporting also takes time. Compare receipt frontiers as well as time.

Mechanism pass requires a graph cell to retain its legitimate pre-materialization
prospective support. Functional follow-through additionally requires that a
new-shell provider participates in an AVAILABLE all-reply envelope, handoff
and nonzero successor value. Report exhaustive development M2 results
separately; a mechanism pass is not a curriculum pass.

No qualifying four-witness candidate within the budget is inconclusive for
transfer, not proof of a broken transfer implementation. Stop on evidence
leakage, source mismatch, inconsistent replay, core mutation or resource/cap
failure. Zero M2 validation remains a no-go for an expanded full campaign.
Preserve and interrupt only the new experiment workers if free disk drops below
1.5 GiB. Do not extend or restart either arm automatically. A negative result
ends this package; it is not permission to tune or add the next mechanism.

## Remaining limits

No convergence or generalization guarantee is claimed. Lifetime audit/index
work is not necessarily history-independent despite bounded active ecology.
Concrete-actuator Q transfer, mixed sampled-terminal/worst-reply credit, and
the incompatible meanings of legacy fast-value/M4 consolidation remain
separate investigations. Running longer is not their repair.

successor value and TD credit; the materializing event cannot bootstrap itself.

tested timing/credit path, not new chess competence or generalization.

compatibility failure found by the broader suite was repaired by validating
