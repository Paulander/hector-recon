# Native prospective evidence authority — preregistration

Status: frozen development-only protocol. This document, implementation, runner, and tests must be committed and pushed before the canonical synthetic stream is generated. A separate manifest then freezes their hashes and the exact historical hypotheses before any canonical arm runs.

## Question and single factor

The historical learner used records both to discover a competence pattern and to mature it. Specialization likewise used a contradiction to nominate a child and immediately reviewed that child on the same accumulated ledger. This package changes one factor: lifecycle authority comes only from distinct grounded REAL receipts strictly after a cell's birth frontier and only when the cell predicted/activated before the outcome arrived.

Alternative hypothesis: prospective authority prevents accidental same-ledger self-certification while preserving some genuinely repeated competence. Null: later evidence is too sparse or the same internal representation remains nonselective, so useful authority does not survive.

This is not held-out generalization, R1, fresh evidence, recursive specialization, a representation change, or a tuning package.

## Frozen architectural law

Every competence cell, including an ordinary candidate or one-level specialization child, stores discovery and certification ledgers separately. Discovery records may nominate a pattern but never certify it. The proposal event and all events at or before the recorded birth frontier are excluded. A distinct later signed grounded REAL receipt counts only when a REAL classification was emitted before its outcome. VIRTUAL frames add zero evidence. Duplicate receipt delivery is idempotent.

Maturity remains exactly four prospective supports, zero prospective contradictions, and the existing one-sided Wilson lower bound (z=1.6448536269514722) at least 0.55. A later contradiction revokes mature authority through the existing graph-local falsification route. The organism—not the runner—owns membership, polarity, counts, thresholding, transition decisions, outcome-shuffle control, and the read-only exact `EVIDENCE_DEFICIT` terminal. The terminal cannot request exposure or schedule a trial in this package.

No replay, repeat identities, threshold reduction, extra capacity/lifetime, new terminals/features, chess labels, ensembles, voting, selection, recursive specialization, or exception topology is permitted.
