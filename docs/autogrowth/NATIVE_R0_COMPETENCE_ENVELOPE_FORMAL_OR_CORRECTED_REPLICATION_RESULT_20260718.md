# Native R0 competence envelope: formal-OR corrected replication result

## Verdict

The bounded instrument repair and corrected replication completed without
opening regression, R1, the retired 65 successors, a fresh pool, or any new KRK
cohort.

Explicit `confirm_policy="or"` is now a race-safe one-of-N contract. The old
V3B/V3C artifacts remain byte-preserved records of the faulty implementation;
they are not estimates of intended OR behavior. Replaying all frozen V3B
organisms through the repaired engine reproduced the preregistered descriptive
expectation exactly. Regrowing all 32 paired seeds from empty envelopes then
passed both original V3B engagement gates. Viewed-validation development still
failed selectivity: the corrected organisms emitted 313 true positives and 39
false positives.

The bounded representation ceiling changes the diagnosis. The representation
contains many training-supported patterns that are safe on the viewed
validation rows, and the frozen genome did attempt, admit, and mature some of
them. They coexist with broader mature cells that remain authoritative after
contradictory real outcomes. The next isolated mechanism should therefore be
continual mature-cell falsification/refinement or graph-local competition, not
more grace, capacity, proposal throughput, or residual-responsibility by
default. That mechanism was not implemented here.

## Ordered next decision

Freeze one narrow engineering/development design in which later real outcomes
can challenge an already-mature competence cell through graph-owned evidence.
The design should separate at least: evidence insertion, contradiction sensing,
confidence/value update, demotion or refinement proposal, and competition
between broader and safer cells. Controls should distinguish continual
falsification from merely adding capacity or replaying more positives. It must
not let the host label target identity, select cells, fabricate reward, or
inject availability.

Do not infer that residual-responsibility is necessary from this package. Do
not open fresh data or R1 until the mature-cell correction mechanism itself has
an authority-closed, touched-data-only causal canary and an externally frozen
scientific package.
