# Native R0 competence envelope: formal-OR corrected replication

## Status and purpose

This is a bounded instrument-repair and corrected-replication package. It
preserves all artifacts through `9d19b28` and uses semantic-repair commit
`7ea7f4317483047a5330a74d826f56d907b9c2a3`.

Hypothesis: when the documented explicit `confirm_policy="or"` contract is
implemented as race-safe one-of-N, the frozen V3B organisms replay to the
predeclared descriptive totals and the unchanged V3B learner still passes its
original engagement/discrimination gates when regrown from scratch.

Strongest null: the replay differs from the predeclared totals, or corrected
from-scratch V3B fails either its original discrimination or reliability gate.
Either null closes the package without rescue.

## Frozen factor and information boundary

The only runtime change is explicit formal-OR semantics. The 64 touched
training rows, 32-seed manifest, outcome permutation, learner source,
thresholds, capacities, lifecycle, three structural rounds, request order,
resource budgets, and paired connected/shuffled arms remain unchanged.

The learner sees only its original active signals and real or frozen-shuffled
outcomes. It cannot see pool names, validation membership, expected classes,
ceiling patterns, or any diagnostic result. No seed retry, organism selection,
ensemble, cell union, voting, threshold change, or omitted organism is allowed.

## Phase 1: frozen-organism repair replay

All old V3B organisms are replayed, without learning, on the already-viewed V3C
validation rows through the unchanged serialized wrapper/dream-session path.
The replay must produce exactly:

- connected 313 TP and 39 FP;
- 31/32 connected organisms with any TP;
- 6/32 connected safe-narrow and 0/32 strict;
- outcome-shuffled 0 TP and 0 FP;
- exact formal-availability and formal-refutation invariants;
- all original parity, mutation, state, and authority gates.

Any discrepancy is a repair-replay abort. Corrected growth must not start.

## Phase 2: corrected V3B

Regenerate all 32 connected and all 32 outcome-shuffled organisms from empty
competence envelopes under the repaired engine. Store them only at new paths.
The original V3B gates are unchanged:

- connected engagement >=24/32;
- shuffled engagement <=6/32;
- connected-only minus shuffled-only >=20;
- reliability requires connected engagement >=28/32.

Failure of mechanism discrimination or reliability closes the package without
validation, representation-ceiling work, or rescue.

## Conditional viewed-validation development

Only if both V3B gates pass, evaluate every corrected organism on the same
already-viewed V3C validation rows. This is development evidence, never sealed
confirmation. Persist every row, confusion matrix, per-row concentration, and
per-cell attribution. Regression never opens regardless of result.

After corrected validation, run a read-only, learner-invisible arity-1–3
representation ceiling over training plus viewed validation signals, excluding
`internal:policy_response`. Integrity expectations are:

- 80 training-pure validation-safe pairs;
- 10,622 training-pure validation-safe triples;
- best zero-FP validation coverage 12/16 for a pair and 14/16 for a triple;
- 102 combined-data pure pairs and 13,265 combined-data pure triples;
- no support>=4 pure negative singleton, pair, or triple.

A training-pure validation-safe pattern has training support >=4, positive-only
training outcomes, at least one validation true positive, and zero validation
false positives. The diagnostic records whether such patterns were attempted,
admitted, or matured, but never feeds them back into learning.

## Firewall, stop, and interpretation

Regression, fresh pools, R1, the retired 65 successors, and every new KRK cohort
remain forbidden. The package does not change representation, terminal
provenance, virtual frames, competence lifecycle, or continual correction.

Final reporting must separate old descriptive replay, corrected growth,
viewed-validation development, representation ceiling, and the unimplemented
boundary that mature cells cannot yet be falsified or refined by later real
outcomes. Recommend that next mechanism if supported; do not implement it here.
