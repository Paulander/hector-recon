# Graph-Native Learned R0 Competence Envelope Design

Date: 2026-07-16. Status: touched-data engineering preregistration frozen. Do not open fresh data,
final pools, R1 learning, or confirmation data. The authorized implementation is bound by the amendment below.

## Objective

Learn a graph-native AVAILABLE signal that predicts whether the mature R0 policy,
when formally requested in the current local context, will complete its own
declared goal with its own selected action. This is an abstention/competence
envelope around the existing policy, not a second move selector.

The diagnostic prerequisite passed: perfect policy-success availability selected
`d8c8` and converted the retired R1 row; shuffled and disconnected controls did
not. Any-response availability failed.

## Constitutional contract

- POLICY_RESPONSE: a child action was formally emitted. It may be used for
  exploration but never implies applicability.
- AVAILABLE: a graph-local outcome-calibrated prediction that this requested
  child script completes now. Only AVAILABLE may feed exploitative all-reply
  handover.
- VALUE: expected return conditional on AVAILABLE.
- GROUNDING: acyclic provenance from earlier real outcomes; it never means the
  child applies to the present state.
- ACTION_OPTION remains `AND(legal actuator, ALL_REPLIES_AVAILABLE)`.
- Zero exploit options yields zero exploit actuators. A separate anonymous graph
  exploration SCRIPT may act, with no host fallback.

## Learner-visible evidence

Candidate-local evidence may include only ordinary graph state already recorded
in the diagnostic artifact:

- active atom identities and activations;
- active mature composite identities;
- selected policy-response strength and margin represented inside the graph;
- local request, confirmation, negative-confirmation, and false-positive history;
- local real completion successes/failures;
- maturity, grounding provenance, and child-owned uncertainty.

It may not include FEN identity, curriculum/stage labels, mate distance, correct
moves, forced-mate predicates, any-action-success, tablebases, Stockfish, the
retired R1 row identity, or the old host prototype probability.

## Proposed topology

For each mature child policy branch, materialize candidate-local internal
terminals for POLICY_RESPONSE and real completion history. A generic competence
SCRIPT composes those terminals with selected active atoms/composites and emits
AVAILABLE. The measurement backend may read graph-owned counters, but Python may
not reconstruct availability or select thresholds at runtime. The AVAILABLE
SCRIPT must serialize with the organism and execute through FormalReConEngine.

Availability learning occurs only after this real sequence:

`request child -> child emits action -> execute action in reality -> environment
reports terminal fact -> declared completion terminal confirms/fails -> update
local competence evidence`.

A noncompletion is a local negative competence example. Unless the environment
separately reports stalemate, rook loss, or another terminal failure, it is not a
chess loss and supplies no fabricated terminal reward. Dreams never update the
envelope or certify themselves.

### Hypothesis, null, and single factor

Hypothesis: a serialized graph-native competence envelope, grown by ordinary
residual-triggered ReCoN structure from unique real R0 completion observations,
can learn selective local applicability around the frozen R0 policy and thereby
supply AVAILABLE to the already-correct fail-closed R0-to-R1 handover.

Strongest null: the permitted graph state and content-blind growth law cannot
separate competent R0 contexts from response-present local failures. Any apparent
conversion is instead global evidence, shuffled identity, laboratory selection,
or host authority.

The sole scientific factor is the competence envelope:

`POLICY_RESPONSE AND matching mature competence-context SCRIPT -> AVAILABLE`.

The R0 action policy, learned topology, weights, consolidated VALUE, retrieval
budget 16, virtual frames, all-reply SCRIPT, parent CHOICE, opponent transitions,
and all R1 learning remain frozen.

### Binding response contract

- POLICY_RESPONSE means the frozen R0 graph emitted one legal action.
- AVAILABLE predicts that this exact emitted policy action completes R0's
  declared request in the present context.
- REFUTED means a mature local context has supported noncompletion.
- UNKNOWN means neither mature positive nor mature refuted context applies, or
  both apply.
- VALUE remains the frozen child return and is consumed only with AVAILABLE.
- GROUNDING is provenance from unique real observations, not applicability.
- Trial context cells are shadow-only and cannot feed handover.
- Dreams, validation, regression, and retired canaries are read-only.

The exploit option remains
`AND(legal actuator, ALL_REPLIES_AVAILABLE)`. Zero qualified exploit options
emits zero exploit actuators. The separate zero-strength route is named
`graph-owned fallback`, not exploration; the laboratory supplies coverage.

### Frozen estimator and evidence identity

A unique evidence key is SHA-256 over canonical pre-action FEN, the graph-emitted
R0 actuator identity, and the declared completion-terminal identity. Each key may
update competence evidence at most once. Repeated scans are structural replay,
not new grounding.

Every context cell serializes its ordered member identities, birth event,
StemCell state, unique evidence keys, successes, failures, support, one-sided
90% Wilson lower bounds using `z = 1.6448536269514722`, conservative success
estimate, uncertainty, maturity decision, and real-outcome provenance.

Frozen decisions:

- minimum maturity support: 4 unique matching evidence keys;
- positive maturity: support at least 4, failures exactly 0, and success Wilson
  lower bound at least 0.55;
- refuted maturity: support at least 4, successes exactly 0, and failure Wilson
  lower bound at least 0.55;
- mixed cells remain trial until the final lifecycle review, then prune;
- trials with support below 4 prune at the final review;
- mature cells are immutable for this package;
- positive and refuted capacity: 32 each;
- trial capacity: 192; total request-opportunity and proposal-attempt caps: 192;
- duplicate or over-capacity proposals are recorded and receive no retry.

At inference, AVAILABLE is the OR of confirming mature positive contexts, unless
a mature refuted context also confirms. REFUTED is the OR of confirming mature
negative contexts, unless a mature positive also confirms. Both or neither is
UNKNOWN. The reported probability is the maximum applicable positive lower
bound, 0 for REFUTED, and 0.5 for UNKNOWN. This probability is diagnostic; the
handover consumes only the formal state.

### AVAILABILITY_ERROR and growth authority

A graph-native internal terminal emits:

`availability_error = observed_completion - predicted_availability`.

For error accounting only, AVAILABLE is 1, REFUTED is 0, and UNKNOWN is 0.5.
False positives, false negatives, and UNKNOWN real outcomes may request growth.
VALUE residual cannot nominate members or structure.

The frozen generic `CompetenceContextGrowthGenome` owns member choice. For
each graph-emitted request it receives only active eligible node identities and
uses BLAKE2b priorities over genome seed, structural round, request ordinal, and
node identity. It never receives outcomes, FEN, row IDs, curriculum labels, mate
facts, validation scores, or Python feature ranks. Python may materialize only
the exact emitted member tuple.

Eligible members are existing active external atom terminals, active mature R0
composites, raw graph-owned internal POLICY_RESPONSE measurements, and mature
competence-context SCRIPTs. No manually authored context member is permitted.

Three fixed structural rounds replay the same immutable 64-event observation
tape without adding grounding:

1. round 0 proposes one-member contexts;
2. round 1 proposes two-member conjunctions;
3. round 2 proposes a recursive mature context plus one active base member when
   one exists, otherwise a three-member base conjunction.

There is exactly one graph request opportunity per event per round: 64 opportunities per round,
192 total. Only a formally confirmed AVAILABILITY_ERROR request consumes a proposal attempt. After each round, every candidate is rescanned against the immutable
unique-evidence ledger and receives one lifecycle review. The final review prunes
all nonmature trials. Selection seed is `2026071606`. Composition is recursive,
content-blind, and never enumerates or injects a known separator. If Python must
choose membership or rank candidates, stop at `growth_authority_failure`.

Banned from learned runtime and fitting:
`OutcomeCalibratedCompetenceGate`,
`OutcomeCalibratedPrototypeGate`, `_fit_r0_gate`, fitted
`_policy_response` features, prototype probabilities, any-action success,
diagnostic response injection, FEN lookup, best-conjunction search, and
validation-selected topology.

### Real observation sequence and mutation firewall

For every training event:

`REAL context -> formal frozen R0 request -> exact R0 actuator executes ->
declared real completion terminal confirms/fails -> responsible matching
competence evidence updates once -> AVAILABILITY_ERROR may request growth`.

Noncompletion is local competence failure, not a chess loss or reward. Only the
envelope subtree may mutate. Before and after digests must prove bit identity of
the frozen R0 graph outside that subtree, R0 VALUE/credit, policy, retrieval
budget, and RNG state. Validation, regression, retired successors, control
shuffles, serialization checks, and dreams must cause zero mutation.

### Frozen metrics and development gates

Validation is read-only:

- decoy false positives exactly 0/16;
- true positives at least 14/16;
- zero mutation.

Regression is one-touch/read-only:

- decoy false positives exactly 0/16;
- true positives at least 14/16;
- validation plus regression positives at least 29/32;
- balanced Brier and selective risk beat constant-available,
  constant-unavailable, global-evidence, and outcome-shuffled controls;
- exact confusion matrices and uncertainty are reported; no broad calibration
  claim is permitted.

Retired 65, read-only:

- exactly the one actual R0 policy-success successor is AVAILABLE;
- no policy-failure successor is AVAILABLE;
- connected handover selects `d8c8` and converts;
- output-shuffled, disconnected, global, forced `d8c8/d8c7` swap, and
  reply-count-matched controls do not reproduce conversion.

Integrity:

- R0 real policy completion remains 32/32;
- R0 graph/value/credit is bit-identical outside the envelope subtree;
- formal AVAILABLE routing and serialization parity pass;
- authority tripwires remain zero;
- dream updates, host gates, host fallback, fabricated grounding, and serialized
  FEN/row identity are zero;
- self-grown competence topology is nonzero.

Balanced Brier is the mean of class-conditional Brier means. Selective risk is
false positives divided by AVAILABLE emissions, with zero emissions reported as
undefined rather than perfect. Constant controls predict 1 and 0; global evidence
predicts the frozen train completion rate 48/64. Thresholds are not fitted.
