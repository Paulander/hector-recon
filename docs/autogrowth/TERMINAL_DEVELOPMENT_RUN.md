# Terminal developmental loop: initial engineering profile

Frozen before the first run, 2026-09-05. This is KRK engineering integration,
not a controlled scientific attribution or autonomy confirmation experiment.

Hypothesis: a persistent graph with generated sparse readers and Boolean
compositions can improve real M1 choices from scalar exercise outcomes. Strongest
null: the finite random proposal grammar and sparse rewards provide inadequate
coverage/credit; graph growth alone will not improve unseen M1 positions.

The changed implementation is the complete observation-to-action path. The old
hybrid is a historical reference, not a matched causal arm. New decision values
must be computed by persistent graph SCRIPTs from terminal confirmations and
plastic SUR edges. No board reconstruction, successor simulation, mate feature,
solution label, teacher scorer, or per-position action-value cache in the learner.
Legal action bindings and current-board/action-intent geometry are embodiment.
The adapter may translate a requested action; it may not select one.

Initial profile: seed 1; at most 96 live condition definitions (instantiated separately per action binding); 2 generic proposals
per rewarded exercise; equality readers over finite typed coordinates; one to
three readers per AND/OR/XOR script; all trials may compose immediately. Fast
normalized reward-error updates, slow transfer that preserves the effective
weight, minimum 256 completed exercises of trial grace. No individual terminal
utility gate. Shared readers survive while any retained composition needs them.

Budget: one run, at most 2,048 real one-move exercises or 180 seconds, whichever
comes first. Evaluate the existing development validation pool before and after;
keep the final test sealed. Do not tune after observing that result. Stop for an
information-boundary violation, illegal output, nonfinite weights, or failed
continuation checks. Negative chess performance is a reportable completed run.

Deterministic mechanism tests are separate from the chess run: zero-marginal XOR
with actions, terminal-mask invariance, selected-path delayed credit, composition
member retention, graph arbitration, and exact continuation after serialization.
No claim of full KRK, a discovered world model, learned temporal planning, or
sealed transfer follows from these tests or this bounded development run.

Implementation-specific interpretation: proposal coordinates, values and Boolean
operators are sampled internally from the declared finite schema. Reward does
not directly move equality targets; selection/pruning changes the represented
reader population. The effective prediction is a linear sum of confirmed
condition contributions, so the requested nonlinear capacity comes from the
learned selection of Boolean compositions. Signed expected-return support is
carried in the weighted SCRIPT activation; input-terminal activation remains a
0/1 match. These are separate quantities, not a claim that value is sensory
activation or calibrated affordance.

No causal MATURE certificate is issued from reward correlation. Repeated
participation enters the existing PROBATION state and permits internal slow
weight transfer. The existing intervention counters remain zero. The run tests
operational survival and reuse, not completed causal lifecycle certification.

## Recorded result

The fixed run stopped at its wall limit after 182 actual moves and 99 mates.
Nonlearning development validation improved from 4/128 to 124/128. No illegal
actions or abstentions; final test unopened. The 96 learned condition definitions
instantiate 3,385 vertices across bindings. Pruning did not occur before the
wall limit; its grace and dependency behavior passed separate tests. See
[the measured report](../../reports/autogrowth/development/TERMINAL_DEVELOPMENT_SMOKE_20260905.json).
No mechanism or training parameter was changed in response to these outcomes.
