# Existing birth-budget probe: 32 versus 64 conditions

Protocol declared after the representation diagnosis and tie-aware certificates,
before this probe's outcomes. Those investigations found no formal-computation
mismatch, but proved that each examined fixed graph lacks a perfect weight policy
on the examined rows. This motivates a small capacity test, not an assertion that
more conditions are the only repair or that 64 will suffice.

Restore the exact **no-addition** episode-1,024 actors for seeds 2 and 3. Clone each
into two arms: keep `max_conditions=32`, or set it to **64**. This is the sole
treatment. No source learner, birth law, pruning rule, exploration probability,
credit law, reward, feature or strategy changes. New conditions are proposed by
the existing generic random birth law and learn from actual scalar outcomes.
The budget gives them room; it supplies no particular terminal or Boolean gate.

Both arms receive the same next **256** ordinary training positions from the
existing seeded schedule, ending at event **1,280**. Separate existing exploration
streams must match. Evaluate each final actor once on the 128 development rows,
with learning disabled. No intermediate performance selects a checkpoint or
changes a setting. Prior diagnostics never supply labels, fitted weights or
selected actions to these actors. The diagnostic module is used only for its
trusted checkpoint loader, outside play; tests forbid its analysis functions.

**1,024 training + 512 evaluation = 1,536 actual moves.** Two seed workers,
**900 seconds per seed**, one attempt; no retry or extension. This is a separate
experiment, not an extension of either completed diagnostic or the earlier
1,024-decision experiment. Save private checkpoints before training, after each
128-decision block and at evaluation; verify final restore. Preserve failures.
No trained weights enter git and the final test stays unopened.

Primary comparison: final 64-budget minus 32-budget mates for each seed. Also
report paired gains/losses, new births/retirements, final live definitions, frozen
history, matched exploration and source/state continuity. These two seeds were
selected during development; no general superiority or adaptive-growth claim.
The unchanged 32-budget arm controls for the extra ordinary play.

Run `recon_lite_chess.experiments.m1_capacity` with the same pool, ordinary-M1
reference and previous public run, `--private-source` pointing to its immutable
private checkpoints, and fresh `--output` and `--private` directories. The focused
test checks the single-field treatment, actual opaque play, disabled diagnostic
functions, matched exploration, added births and restored checkpoint state.

## Completed result — 2026-09-07

The diagnosis evidence and this protocol/implementation were published before
play at `3c0886132e25114f3ec1dca83a6ca6b43aa21c31`. All 74 focused checks passed
before the probe. After all work, the complete required branch suite passed
**194 tests**. The [public aggregate](../../reports/autogrowth/development/M1_CAPACITY_PROBE_20260907.json)
records the sole-field treatment, source/checkpoint identity, fixed schedules,
complete outcomes, lifecycle observations and final checkpoint hashes.

Both seeds completed all **1,536 declared actual moves** in one attempt: 1,024
training and 512 frozen evaluation, with zero laboratory labels entering the
learner. Seed times were 508.044 and 522.024 seconds, within the 900-second caps.
Both inherited actors stayed unchanged; their digests match the original saved
actors before the budget treatment. All four final checkpoints restored exactly.
Both exploration pairs and all four frozen shadow histories matched; all four
evaluations preserved learned state. Sixteen immutable private payloads were
retained. No final-test access, retry or extension occurred.

### What we learned and corrected

The earlier certificates concern **particular fixed graphs**. Ordinary training
changes both graph and weights. The 32-budget seed 3 control demonstrates that
the existing process can escape the observed plateau without a larger budget or
a new mechanism. The certificates never established 66 as an optimal score;
do not convert them into that claim or a reason to replace the learner.

More random capacity helped one selected development case and tied the other.
This establishes a narrow benefit of that budget intervention in seed 2, not
reliable experience-guided structural selection. The seed 3 improvement also
does not isolate the causal contribution of its new births versus continued
weight learning. No adaptive-growth, retention, maturity, handover or M1 mastery
claim follows from these two reused development seeds.
