# External Expert Handoff: Post-Audit Generic-Core Work

Date: 2026-07-12. Branch:
`codex/native-krk-resume-composition`. Head before this handoff:
`75871b1`.

## Executive verdict

The external audit's central criticism was correct: earlier branch progress
mostly improved the human/LLM/Python/ReCoN laboratory while leaving ReCoN's
developmental ownership unidentified.

This work produced two narrow but real improvements:

1. a learner-local residual law discovered and causally retained useful
   anonymous conjunction topology against a budget-matched random proposer;
2. that unchanged law was connected to weighted ReCoN graph action outputs and
   terminal-only delayed responsibility, producing behavior that causally
   depended on grown AND scripts.

It did not establish end-to-end autonomous ReCoN, general cognition, or KRK
closure. The next robust-choice package failed because its nominal mean control
was not an empirical mean after bounded-memory compression. Work stopped there
without repair or rerun. The PI subsequently authorized a separate estimator
repair package; its fresh-seed comparison passed every frozen gate.

## What ReCoN now owns in the positive generic package

- Anonymous primitive terminal weights update from selected-action terminal
  valence.
- The exact weighted graph sum used to choose an action is the prediction
  updated by learning; measured mismatches were zero.
- Learner-local pair support and residual contrast nominate new conjunctions.
- Trial conjunctions train in shadow and have no action-root edge.
- Future paired error minus resource cost decides maturity or pruning.
- Mature conjunctions become real AND scripts with weighted SUB edges.
- Persistent selected activation responsibility carries terminal credit across
  four elapsed step events.
- Disabling mature composite edges changes the chosen behavior substantially.

## What the laboratory still owns

- Literal/object representation and legal action identities.
- Episode boundaries and scalar goal valence.
- Fixed epsilon, learning rates, memory sizes, operator family, and candidate
  budget.
- Task distribution and experience order.
- The distinction between action channels.
- Evaluation, hidden signal diagnostics, and all scientific approval.
- KRK curriculum order, position generation, and chess embodiment.

The delayed package has no changing intermediate state, additional observation,
or later choice. It is a delayed contextual bandit, not a real multi-state
trajectory. It does not establish option discovery, routing, dreaming,
imagination, self-selected curriculum, or planning across a gnome gap.

## Recurrent misses in this branch

1. **The curriculum doctrine was rediscovered instead of enforced.** KRK must
   grow outward at high resolution on one persistent graph: Mate-in-1,
   Mate-in-2 handoff, same-side/tempo, killbox, then fence/approach. Broad pools
   repeatedly hid missing local selectivity.
2. **Failure retirement became failure exclusion.** Exposed rows should leave
   sealed confirmation but remain in learner counterexample replay. Removing
   them and symmetries from training changed the problem.
3. **Names outran semantics.** `real rollout`, `native`, `causal`, and
   `self-growing` were sometimes asserted by flags or helper structure rather
   than distinct behavior.
4. **The value updated differed from the value choosing.** Broad credit then
   reinforced correlations without repairing the actual policy output.
5. **Expected-reply learning was compared with worst-reply evaluation.** A
   sampled mean cannot certify minimax safety.
6. **Self-authored tests stayed below failure regimes.** The rare-refutation
   unit used eight observations; repeated compression after capacity 256
   corrupted the later mean control.
7. **Builder, runner, analyst, and adjudicator collapsed into one agent.**
   Plausible nulls became automatic next patches.
8. **Too many adaptive factors surrounded small samples.** Feature, pool,
   routing, credit, topology, and gate changes made contribution unidentifiable.

## Robust-memory blocker

`RobustReturnMemory._compress` retains the lowest 10% plus deterministic
coverage. That is reasonable for a lower-tail sketch. It is not a valid buffer
from which to compute an empirical mean after repeated compression.

In the failed frozen artifact, the mean arm observed the refutable action
687–758 times but its retained buffers contained 33.2%–39.1% negative returns
from an exactly 12.5%-negative environment. Its reported refutable means fell to
0.21875–0.33594 instead of +0.75. Thus both arms were downside-sensitive.

This invalidates the control, not the raw parity instrumentation. It also means
the earlier eight-sample component result must not be extrapolated to bounded
long-run mean behavior.

## PI decision and completed repair

The package kill rule blocked automatic repair. The PI authorized one new
estimator work package—not a rerun:

1. exact streaming mean from total sum/count;
2. separate bounded lower-tail sketch;
3. confidence from total observation count;
4. capacity-crossing calibration at 256, 1,024, and 4,096 observations;
5. matched constant, Bernoulli, rare-refutation, and distribution-shift streams;
6. fresh preregistration, source commit, seeds, and independent adjudication.

That package passed on fresh seeds. Exact running sum/count is now separate from
the bounded conservative exception sketch. The failed predecessor remains
unchanged and its verdict remains instrument failure.

The PI also authorized the conditional next step: integrate robust value with the already-positive
graph-composition/delayed-credit policy in a genuine multi-state randomized
key-door environment with identity permutation and a mid-run dynamics change.
Require multiple observations and choices, grown-topology ablation, exception
retention, and transfer without hyperparameter changes.

The first key-door package isolated multi-state responsibility rather than
mixing a new robust/contextual combination formula. It falsified retention:
persistent key accuracy was 1.0 in both regimes and new-regime joint success was
1.0, but old-regime door accuracy and joint success were exactly 0.0. All door
candidate slots were born by observation 512 in regime 0; no regime-1 cue
composite could be born. See
`GENERIC_CORE_MULTISTATE_KEY_DOOR_RESULT_20260712.md`.

This strengthens the temporal-credit result while exposing renewable topology
and local consolidation/replay as the next blockers. It does not authorize KRK
transfer.

The PI-authorized renewable-topology package then formed regime-1 cue
composites on 20/20 fresh tasks under a verified four-live/64-total budget.
New-regime success remained 1.0 and topology ablation increased, but median
old-regime success remained 0.0. Structural renewal works and is insufficient;
shared-weight consolidation is now the isolated blocker. See
`GENERIC_CORE_RENEWABLE_TOPOLOGY_RESULT_20260712.md`.

Only after frozen generic integration and independent reproduction should the
release return to fresh KRK pools. At that point use the central high-resolution
outward curriculum as experience scheduling, while keeping stage labels,
tablebase answers, and known-state verdicts outside learner-visible causes.
