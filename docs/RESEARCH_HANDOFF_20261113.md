## Chess

- **Published M1.** Eight fresh selective actors scored 1,512/1,512 on every
  scheduled census from action 1,536 to 3,072 with learning and development
  enabled. [The milestone](NATIVE_M1_REPLICATION_20260928.md) documents the known
  KRK population, replay, technical rerun amendment and unresolved original
  resource receipts. Fresh learning uses the selective factory; the packaged
  seed-61 actor is a separate learned reference. These are sampled retention
  results, with no claim about unseen positions, M2 or lifelong retention.
- **Sparse terminal discovery.** The first isolated pilot began with five
  control nodes, no readers or scoring conditions and a zero bias. It reached
  1,500/1,512 at 3,072 and lost 12 earlier successes. In a fork of that prefix,
  the .1 learning-rate arm reached 1,512/1,512 at 3,072 versus .3's 1,500, with a
  higher cumulative whole-population failure burden. A fresh independent prefix
  later reached 1,512/1,512 at 1,536 using .3; both forks then stayed perfect
  through all scheduled censuses at 4,096. That second run confirms bounded
  minimal-start acquisition and sampled retention; it did not meet the strict
  rate-benefit gate because the control had no losses to recover. Keep .3.
  A declared geometric feature port and generic discovery laws were supplied.
- **Modular Setup → M1.** The verified 256-episode pilot's frozen and plastic
  arms both finished 1,512/1,512 in standalone and parent-mediated M1. Both
  scored 0/32 greedy all-reply M2 at every census. Each saw two rewarded
  Setup → M1 training completions, but Setup remained bias-only after 62/59
  observations, short of its first 64-observation growth boundary. The strict
  child-dependence selector was empty in both arms (NOT_ASSESSED). This is a
  negative result for this joint practice schedule and one reused lineage.
- **Fixed512 continuation: partial technical closure.** Both producers
  completed the fixed 512 additional episodes per arm. Independent own-choice
  reconstruction, checkpoint certificates and census/control stages completed.
  Both semantic verification workers stopped before entering the verifier
  when 330 extra bytecode-cache entries violated the runtime closure. Saved
  observations report endpoint all-reply M2 of 0/32 frozen and 9/32 plastic
  (eight baseline-new strict Setup → M1 boards for plastic), with standalone M1
  of 1,512/1,512 and 1,368/1,512 and parent-mediated M1 of 1,488/1,512 and
  1,368/1,512. These values are provisional, show safety regressions in the
  saved interpretation, and do not establish Setup competence, a practical
  benefit, or child dependence. No complete scientific verdict or archive seal
  was issued. M1 → M1 can be a legitimate general M2 completion; strict
  Setup → M1 measures a separate handover claim. The technical stop remains
  in force.

## Active vision lessons for chess

Active vision is a separate repository and owns its retinal interface, leases,
children and experiments. Separate practice yielded useful recovery and
stabilization under supplied handovers; that did not establish learned
coordination. The subsequent parent with frozen children missed its joint
criterion. In its shared-credit audit, 219/223 blank-start recovery successes
left the immediate recovery–stabilization margin unchanged: the active terms
credited on those events scored both options.

Separate skill coefficients raised continuation visibility from 88.08% to
93.54% on the matched panel, but the joint criterion failed, including a
20.54-percentage-point adaptation loss in one triplet. On matched first-loss
forks, supplying both recovery and stabilization improved the equal-weight
eight-triplet mean by 2.050 visible ticks per 16, but harmed triplet 07 and
missed the adoption gate. The completed component study used all 556 saved
forks and 9,110 new physical ticks. Supplying the first recovery request alone
gained 0.581 ticks [0.225, 0.946] over learned behavior; adding stabilization
at release gained another 1.469 [1.353, 1.586]. Effects varied by triplet;
triplet 07's harm came from the initial request, not stabilization.

The subsequent saved-data audit used 556 loss and 252 release decisions with
zero new physical ticks. In triplet 02, 138 blank loss decisions and 18 visible
releases shared the same admitted activation profile and scores despite different
raw retinal inputs. Reweighting that dictionary alone cannot rank the options
differently in those two contexts. In 07, the existing dictionary already
distinguished a subgroup where the tested recovery beat the learned request,
but ranked recovery below it. The audit separates representation from ranking;
outcome variation under identical observations does not prove expected value is
unlearnable. Controller provenance and hidden world state stay outside parent
scoring. At this handoff, a separately authorized fresh matched lease-value
diagnostic is running with frozen parents and children and a zero-weight shadow
reader. It produces no training updates and has no accepted benefit result yet.

The transferable lesson is to establish child competence, measure the parent's
actual decision opportunities and score margins, and separate what is observable
from what the current readers represent. Keep both projects' policies and
environment interfaces in their own repositories.
